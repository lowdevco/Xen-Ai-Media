What is the core focus of Xen AI Media? +

Xen AI Media is a specialized creative studio based in Sulthan Bathery, Wayanad. We focus on AI-powered video production, dynamic short-form content, and generative visual assets, blending cinematic creativity with advanced AI automation to deliver futuristic media solutions.

Can custom AI models be trained on specific brand assets? +

Yes. We develop and train custom AI models to generate lifelike AI avatars and assets that strictly adhere to your existing brand guidelines, color palettes, and corporate tone of voice.

What is the typical turnaround time for AI video production? +

By leveraging AI automation for scriptwriting, voiceovers, and editing workflows, our production cycles are significantly faster than traditional media. Depending on project complexity, many campaigns and social media assets are finalized within 24 to 48 hours.

Is social media management and automation included in the services? +

Beyond content creation, we offer comprehensive social media automation services. This encompasses AI-generated copywriting, trend detection, strategic content planning, and automated publishing to ensure consistent audience engagement.
